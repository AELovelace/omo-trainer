How your estimate works

Drink totals cover the time since your previous check-in. The model approximates their timing and tests 30-, 60- and 120-minute response half-lives. These are diary-model assumptions, not measurements of absorption or bladder filling. Missing records, sleep, activity and medicines can change the pattern. The model learns only from this profile's records, works offline, and recalculates after edits or sync.

This estimates recorded wettings, not when your body needs to go. Use the bathroom whenever you need; don't wait for the estimate or change your drinking to match it.

      Your LiD0llID account

      Sign in to connect this device.

      Connecting uploads existing entries and future changes to lidoll.dev's central database. The organizer can view and export synced records for later analysis. Other participants cannot see your history.

      New here? Create a shared LiD0llID account with a username and password, then connect this device to sync your entries.

      Entries are on this device until connected.

      A shared account works across lidoll.dev apps. Each app keeps its own data and permissions. Signing out here ends the Little Log session; your shared LiD0llID sign-in may still be active.

      Connected games

      Use your online LiDollCoin balance in LiDollQuest. Review a game connection code or disconnect an app.

      Manage connected games

      Receive notifications

      Receive optional potty reminders and messages from Little Log admins, even when the app is closed. Notifications may appear on your lock screen.

      Potty reminders use your recorded intervals. Use the bathroom whenever you need; reminders are only estimates.

      Browser timezone:

      Matching start and end times turn quiet hours off. These settings apply to all your devices.

      Open Settings while signed in to manage notifications.

      Default position

      Used for rolls and wetting events on this device. You can change the position before saving either.

      Roll probability follows the daily protocol automatically, starting at 50% when you save your first saved record.

      Keep a copy

      Connected accounts sync their entries to the server. Offline changes wait on this device until it reconnects. Clearing browser data before syncing loses those pending changes.

      A JSON backup includes entries and local settings, without login credentials. Import merges new entries and queues them for sync when connected. CSV is for spreadsheets.

      Backups contain personal records. Store them somewhere private.

      Take the terminal with you

      On iPhone or iPad: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android or desktop: use your browser's Install app option.

      After the first successful visit, logging works offline. Signing in and syncing need an internet connection. Installation requires HTTPS (or localhost for development).

      Checking offline support…

      Delete your entries

      When connected, deleting entries also deletes them from the central database after syncing. Offline devices receive those deletions on their next sync. Previously exported files or server backups may still contain older copies.

      Use Sign out & clear device to remove only this device's copy. Download a backup before deleting anything you want to keep.

      CHRYSALIS // ACTIVE PROTOCOL

      Daily probability

      50%

      Each completed day: F + SF + V ≥ SI + I decreases the chance by 5 percentage points. F + SF + V < SI + I increases it by 5. A day with no wettings recorded also increases it by 5. Bounds: 20–80%.
