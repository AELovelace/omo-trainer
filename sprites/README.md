# Sticker collection

The active reward collection has 37 stickers: 12 unique numbered PNGs, 9 animals (`animal_01.png` through `animal_09.png`), and 16 suns (`sun_01.png` through `sun_16.png`). Every synced observation, wetting, or diaper change draws one random sticker from this full collection with equal chances. `_originals/` contains backup source copies and is excluded from rewards and the public asset allowlist.

Additional PNG, WebP, JPEG and GIF images can be added here, including nested folders. Restart Little Log after changes. Keep published relative filenames stable: they determine sticker IDs and ownership history. Numbered assets appear as Sticker 1, Sticker 2, and so on. Animals and suns appear as Animal Sticker 1 and Sun Sticker 1, respectively. These sets use the same ownership counts, reward modal, gallery, market and stickerbank inventory as the original collection. Removed assets retire their types without deleting balances.

Duplicate scans 13.png, 14.png, 15.png and 16.png repeat designs 1.png, 2.png, 3.png and 4.png. They are retained as source files but excluded from rewards and public serving. The market merges existing duplicate holdings into the original IDs without removing owned quantities.
