# Sticker collection

The 12 unique numbered PNG images are the active reward collection. `_originals/` contains backup source copies and is excluded from rewards and the public asset allowlist.

Additional PNG, WebP, JPEG and GIF images can be added here, including nested folders. Restart Little Log after changes. Keep published relative filenames stable: they determine sticker IDs and ownership history. Numbered assets appear as Sticker 1, Sticker 2, and so on. Removed assets retire their types without deleting balances.

Duplicate scans 13.png, 14.png, 15.png and 16.png repeat designs 1.png, 2.png, 3.png and 4.png. They are retained as source files but excluded from rewards and public serving. The market merges existing duplicate holdings into the original IDs without removing owned quantities.
